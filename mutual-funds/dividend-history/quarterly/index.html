Quarterly Dividend History (IDCW)

A quarterly IDCW option can declare IDCW once a quarter. Each declaration, and its amount, depends on the scheme's distributable surplus at the time, so a quarter can pass with no payout.

62 IDCW options from 20 fund houses have a quarterly frequency in our data, and 41 of them paid IDCW in the 12 months to 18 Aug 2025. The most common categories are Equity Savings Funds (15), Conservative Hybrid Funds (15) and Arbitrage Funds (6).

About IDCW

IDCW amounts can be distributed out of investors' capital (Equalization Reserve), which is part of the sale price that represents realised gains.

Past IDCW is not an indication of future IDCW. IDCW is not guaranteed and depends on distributable surplus.

IDCW is added to your income and taxed at your slab rate, with TDS of 10% on IDCW above ₹10,000 a year from one fund house.
