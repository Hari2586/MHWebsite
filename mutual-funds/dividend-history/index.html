About IDCW

IDCW amounts can be distributed out of investors' capital (Equalization Reserve), which is part of the sale price that represents realised gains.

Past IDCW is not an indication of future IDCW. IDCW is not guaranteed and depends on distributable surplus.

IDCW is added to your income and taxed at your slab rate, with TDS of 10% on IDCW above ₹10,000 a year from one fund house.
