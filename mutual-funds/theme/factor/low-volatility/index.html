Low Volatility Factor Funds

Low volatility funds hold the stocks whose prices have moved least in the past, aiming for a smoother ride than the market. They have tended to fall less in sell-offs and rise less in sharp rallies.

There are 18 schemes on this page from 9 fund houses; the largest by assets is ICICI Prudential Equity Minimum Variance Fund at ₹2,919.84 Crores, as on 31 Aug 2026. They come from the SEBI categories Equity Index Fund (10), Exchange Traded Fund (4), Sectoral or Thematic Fund (2) and Fund of Funds (2).

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Low Volatility Factor Funds are there?

There are 18 Low Volatility Factor Funds on this page as on 31 Aug 2026: 2 actively managed and 16 index funds, ETFs or funds of funds, counting one Regular Plan – Growth option per scheme.

What is the difference between the active funds and the index funds here?

An active fund's manager chooses which stocks to hold within the theme and can differ from any index; an index fund or ETF holds the stocks of one published index in its weights. Index funds usually cost less; active funds may do better or worse than the index.

How are Low Volatility Factor Funds taxed?

All 18 schemes are classed Equity-Oriented for tax. Each scheme page has a Taxation section with the rules that apply.
