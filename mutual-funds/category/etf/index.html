How Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) Are Taxed

The 132 schemes fall into 2 tax classes: 119 Equity-Oriented and 13 Other. Equity-Oriented: Gains on units held up to 12 months are taxed at 20%; after 12 months, gains above ₹1,25,000 a year are taxed at 12.5%. Other: Gains are taxed at your slab rate if held up to 24 months (12 months for listed units), and at 12.5% after that, with no exemption. Each scheme page has a Taxation section with the rules that apply.

Based on income-tax rules in force on 29 Sep 2026. This is general information, not tax advice. Please consult a tax professional.
