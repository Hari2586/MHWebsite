How Debt Index Funds Are Taxed

All 105 schemes are classed Debt-Oriented (Slab Rate) for tax. Gains on units bought on or after 1 Apr 2023 are taxed at your income-tax slab rate, however long you hold them. Each scheme page has a Taxation section with the rules that apply.

Based on income-tax rules in force on 29 Sep 2026. This is general information, not tax advice. Please consult a tax professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Debt Index Funds are there?

There are 105 Debt Index Funds from 17 fund houses, as on 31 Aug 2026, counting one Regular Plan – Growth option per scheme.

What does SEBI require of a Debt Index Fund?

Under SEBI's categorisation rules, a Debt Index Fund tracks a debt index; most in this group are target maturity funds that end on a fixed date.

How are Debt Index Funds taxed?

All 105 schemes are classed Debt-Oriented (Slab Rate) for tax. The class depends on how much of the portfolio is in Indian listed shares or in debt; each scheme's page shows its class and the rules.

What is the expense ratio range in this category?

Regular-plan expense ratios range from 0.09% to 1.01%, as on 31 Aug 2026. The Direct plan of the same scheme costs less because it pays no distributor commission.
