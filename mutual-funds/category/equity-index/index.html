How Equity Index Funds Are Taxed

The 245 schemes fall into 2 tax classes: 244 Equity-Oriented and 1 Other. Equity-Oriented: Gains on units held up to 12 months are taxed at 20%; after 12 months, gains above ₹1,25,000 a year are taxed at 12.5%. Other: Gains are taxed at your slab rate if held up to 24 months (12 months for listed units), and at 12.5% after that, with no exemption. Each scheme page has a Taxation section with the rules that apply.

Based on income-tax rules in force on 29 Sep 2026. This is general information, not tax advice. Please consult a tax professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

There are 245 Equity Index Funds from 26 fund houses, as on 31 Aug 2026, counting one Regular Plan – Growth option per scheme.

What does SEBI require of a Equity Index Fund?

Under SEBI's categorisation rules, a Equity Index Fund tracks an equity index by holding the same stocks in the same weights.

How are Equity Index Funds taxed?

The 245 schemes fall into 2 tax classes: 244 Equity-Oriented and 1 Other. The class depends on how much of the portfolio is in Indian listed shares or in debt; each scheme's page shows its class and the rules.
