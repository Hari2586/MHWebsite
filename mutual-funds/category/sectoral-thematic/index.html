How Sectoral and Thematic Funds Are Taxed

The 302 schemes fall into 3 tax classes: 243 Equity-Oriented, 57 Other and 2 Not Yet Confirmed. Equity-Oriented: Gains on units held up to 12 months are taxed at 20%; after 12 months, gains above ₹1,25,000 a year are taxed at 12.5%. Other: Gains are taxed at your slab rate if held up to 24 months (12 months for listed units), and at 12.5% after that, with no exemption. Each scheme page has a Taxation section with the rules that apply.

Based on income-tax rules in force on 29 Sep 2026. This is general information, not tax advice. Please consult a tax professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Sectoral and Thematic Funds are there?

There are 302 Sectoral and Thematic Funds from 38 fund houses, as on 31 Aug 2026, counting one Regular Plan – Growth option per scheme.

What does SEBI require of a Sectoral or Thematic Fund?

Under SEBI's categorisation rules, a Sectoral or Thematic Fund invests at least 80% in one sector or theme.

How are Sectoral and Thematic Funds taxed?

The 302 schemes fall into 3 tax classes: 243 Equity-Oriented, 57 Other and 2 Not Yet Confirmed. The class depends on how much of the portfolio is in Indian listed shares or in debt; each scheme's page shows its class and the rules.

What is the expense ratio range in this category?
