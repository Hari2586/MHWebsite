MF/SIF Screener

Illustration – modelled data. The performance, risk, SIP, SWP and peer figures on this page are computed from modelled NAV histories, not the schemes’ actual NAVs, to demonstrate how the screener works. They do not describe the real performance of any scheme. Scheme names, categories, fund houses, AUM and costs are from AMFI data.

Data Sources. AMFI, CAMS, KFintech.

Assumptions. Returns below one year are absolute, one year and above are annualised. SIP ₹10,000/month; top-up 10% a year; SWP ₹10,00,000 invested, 6% withdrawn a year, stepped up 6% a year. Category averages are computed within category and are not shown below 5 schemes with a value.

Disclaimer. Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully. Past performance may or may not be sustained in future. Nothing here is investment advice.
