ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund has 129 open schemes across 39 SEBI categories, as on 31 Aug 2026.

Who is the trustee of ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund?

The trustee company is ICICI Prudential Trust Limited. SEBI registration number: MF/003/93/6.

Who is the registrar (RTA) for ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund?

Computer Age Management Services Limited handles investor records, statements and transaction processing for ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund.

How do I start, stop or change a SIP with ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund?

Through your distributor, the fund house's website or app, or the registrar (Computer Age Management Services Limited). A SIP can be stopped at any time without charge; units already bought stay invested.
