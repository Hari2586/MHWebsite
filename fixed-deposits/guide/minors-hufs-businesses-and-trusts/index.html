FDs for Minors, HUFs, Businesses and Trusts

An FD can be held by more than individuals. Which of the 8 FDs are open, and what changes, depend on who is investing.

Rules as on 3 Oct 2026; tax rules as on 29 Sep 2026.

Minors

A parent or guardian invests on the minor's behalf, and all 8 FDs take deposits for minors. Banks may let a minor aged 10 or more operate the deposit. Senior citizen rates do not apply.

See the FDs open to minors in the FD Explorer

HUFs

The karta invests for the Hindu Undivided Family, using the HUF's PAN. Seven of the 8 FDs take HUF deposits; contact us about Utkarsh Small Finance Bank. Banks do not pay senior citizen rates to HUFs.

See the FDs open to HUFs in the FD Explorer

Businesses

  • Sole proprietorship: the deposit is in the proprietor's name. At a bank, it is added to the proprietor's own deposits for DICGC cover.
  • Partnership firms, LLPs and companies invest through authorised signatories named in a deed or resolution.
  • All 8 FDs take deposits from proprietorships, firms and companies. Five take LLP deposits; contact us about Bajaj Finance, Shriram Finance and Utkarsh Small Finance Bank.
  • Firms, LLPs and companies cannot submit Form 121, so tax is deducted above the threshold.

See the FDs open to companies in the FD Explorer

Charitable and Religious Trusts

Section 350 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, read with Schedule XVI, lists where a charitable or religious trust may invest. It includes deposits with scheduled banks and with housing finance companies that meet its conditions, but not deposits with ordinary NBFCs. A deposit outside these modes can make the trust's income taxable.

Government Bodies and Others

Among the FDs we offer, only LIC Housing Finance takes deposits from government agencies, local authorities and artificial juridical persons through MoneyHoney.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a company put money in an NBFC FD?

Yes. All 8 FDs take company deposits; LIC Housing Finance does so through its Corporate Deposit scheme, so contact us for its rate.

Who is covered by DICGC when a trust holds a bank FD?

Deposits held as trustee are a different capacity, so they are insured separately from the trustee's own deposits, up to ₹5 lakh.

Bank FDs and company FDs differ. Bank deposits are insured by DICGC up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank; company deposits are not insured and depend on the company's ability to repay. Ratings, lock-in, early-withdrawal terms and TDS thresholds also differ. Compare these, not only the rate.

Complaints lie with the company first, then with the RBI Ombudsman for NBFCs and banks, or the National Housing Bank for housing finance companies.
