Bank FDs and Company FDs

Bank FDs and company FDs differ. Bank deposits are insured by DICGC up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank; company deposits are not insured and depend on the company's ability to repay. Ratings, lock-in, early-withdrawal terms and TDS thresholds also differ. Compare these, not only the rate.

FeatureBank FDCompany FD (NBFC or Housing Finance Company)
Deposit insuranceDICGC, up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bankNot insured
RegulatorReserve Bank of IndiaReserve Bank of India; housing finance companies are also supervised by the National Housing Bank
Credit ratingNot a condition for taking depositsAt least BBB- needed to take deposits
Tenure7 days to 10 years (each bank sets its range)12 to 60 months
Early withdrawalThe rate for the period run, less a penaltyLocked in for 3 months; after that a lower rate
Tax deducted (TDS)10% once interest exceeds ₹50,000 a year (₹1,00,000 for senior citizens)10% once interest exceeds ₹10,000 a year

Rates can change. The rate on the day you invest applies.
