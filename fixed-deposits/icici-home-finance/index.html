When Interest Is Paid

Cumulative deposits compound once a year, with simple interest for any months after the last full year. Payouts are credited on these dates:

  • Monthly: last day of each month.
  • Quarterly: 30 Jun, 30 Sep, 31 Dec and 31 Mar.
  • Yearly: 31 Mar.

Early Withdrawal

The Deposit Has RunWhat You Get
First 3 monthsNot allowed, except on the depositor's death (with interest at the contracted rate); for an emergency, up to 50% of the principal or ₹5 lakh, whichever is lower; for a critical illness, the whole principal; and deposits of ₹10,000 or less, in full. Emergency and critical-illness payouts carry no interest.
3 to 6 monthsIndividual depositors: interest at ICICI Bank's minimum savings rate, at most 4% p.a. Other depositors: principal only, no interest.
After 6 monthsInterest at 1% below the rate for the period the deposit ran (2% below the lowest rate if there is no rate for that period).

Before Maturity

  • Renewal instructions: 7 working days before maturity.
  • Repayment instructions: 7 working days before maturity.
  • A loan of up to 75% of the deposit, after 3 months, at 2% above the deposit's rate. Not for minors' or NRI deposits.
  • Up to 3 joint holders.

Tax

Interest is taxable. Tax is deducted at 10% once interest from the company exceeds ₹10,000 in a financial year (20% without PAN). If your tax is nil, you can submit Form 121, which replaced Forms 15G and 15H.

NRIs can invest only from an NRO account, on a non-repatriation basis, for 12 to 36 months. For NRIs, tax is deducted at 30% plus cess on NRO and company FD interest; NRE interest is tax-free. NRIs cannot submit Form 121.

Deposits with ICICI Home Finance Company Limited are a permitted investment for charitable and religious trusts under section 350 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, read with Schedule XVI.

About ICICI Home Finance

ICICI Home Finance Company Limited is a deposit-taking housing finance company, wholly owned by ICICI Bank, regulated by the Reserve Bank of India and supervised by the National Housing Bank. It lends mainly to individuals buying, building or improving homes, and also offers loans against property, construction finance and gold loans.

A fixed deposit with ICICI Home Finance Company Limited is an unsecured deposit with a housing finance company regulated by the Reserve Bank of India and supervised by the National Housing Bank. It is not a bank deposit and is not insured by the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC). Interest and repayment depend on the company's ability to pay. Its rating (Crisil AAA/Stable) is the rating agency's opinion and can change. Money is locked in for the first 3 months except in limited cases, and early withdrawal after that pays a lower rate. Interest is taxable.

The Reserve Bank of India and the National Housing Bank do not accept any responsibility or guarantee about the financial soundness of the company, the correctness of its statements, or the repayment of deposits.

MoneyHoney is an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor. Fixed deposits are not mutual fund products, and AMFI and SEBI grievance mechanisms do not apply to them. Complaints lie with the company first, then with the RBI Ombudsman for NBFCs and banks, or the National Housing Bank for housing finance companies.
