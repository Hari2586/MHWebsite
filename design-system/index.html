MoneyHoney Sunrise

The logo's navy and orange on warm paper. An editorial serif for the human voice, a clear grotesque for the interface, tactile cards with a hard offset edge, and one orange button for the one thing to do. Modelled on Betterment's design and philosophy, built for an Indian mutual fund distributor.

Colour

The website wears the logo: logo navy #16205B for text, links and dark bands; logo orange #FF4A00 for the one primary action, marks and the sun, with ink text on it (5.3:1); their shades and tints for everything else, on warm paper.
