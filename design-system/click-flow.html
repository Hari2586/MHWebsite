MoneyHoney Sunrise · navigation and click flow

Easier to find, one way in.

Eleven flat links become four grouped menus, as Betterment does it. Every page ends on the same orange Get Started, and Get Started asks one question before it hands over to the platform.

The funnel

1Entry

Search, WhatsApp share, a Business Associate, a blog post

2Choose a door

Audience bar (Investors, NRIs, Business Associates) or a menu: Invest, Save, Plan, Learn

3Read the facts

Hub, list, scheme or issue page; a calculator or a solution

4Get Started

One orange button on every page and in the header

5Say what you want

Get Started page: SIP, lump sum, ELSS, FD, NCD, RBI Bond, CGB, SIF, GIFT City, a goal, or talk to us

6Platform

inv.moneyhoney.co.in registration, with the intent passed along (proposal)

Information architecture

Each menu has two titled columns with a one-line description per link, and one promo that answers "where do I start?". Talk to Us is a plain link beside them.

Before and after

Today (Clear Glass)

11 links in one bar: Solutions, Mutual Funds, SIF, GIFT City, Fixed Deposits, Bonds & NCDs, Explore Funds, Fund Screens, Calculators, NFOs & Dividends, Videos. Sign In in the company row. Primary actions point straight to the platform's registration page.

Sunrise

Audience bar: Investors, NRIs, Business Associates. Four menus by intent: Invest, Save, Plan, Learn, plus Talk to Us. Sign In as a quiet text link, Get Started as the one orange button. Every primary action goes to the Get Started page, which records what the visitor wants before registration.
