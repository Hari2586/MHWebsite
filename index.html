Common questions. Plain answers.

Is MoneyHoney an investment adviser?

No. We are a mutual fund distributor, not a SEBI-registered investment adviser. Any suggestion we make is incidental to distribution and limited to mutual fund schemes and SIF strategies, based on our assessment of your risk profile.

Do you charge me a fee?

No. We arrange investments in the Regular Plans of mutual fund schemes and in SIF strategies, and fund houses pay us trail commission on them. The rates are on our Commission Disclosure page.

How do I start?

Choose what you want to do on the Get Started page, create your account on the investor platform and complete KYC. Our team answers phone, WhatsApp and email Monday to Saturday.

Where do the figures on this site come from?

From AMFI and fund house disclosures. Each page shows its source and date, and the Methodology page explains how the data is gathered.
