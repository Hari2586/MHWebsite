How the FD Calculator Works

The rate is the company's printed card rate for the scheme, tenure and payout you pick, for your investor type. Extras are added the way the card adds them: a separate senior citizen card (Bajaj Finance, Shriram Finance, Utkarsh Small Finance Bank), a flat extra (ICICI Home Finance, LIC Housing Finance, PNB Housing Finance), an extra that depends on the tenure (Mahindra Finance), or an extra on the quarterly rate (HDFC Bank).

A cumulative deposit grows by each company's own method:

Bajaj Finance, Mahindra Finance: amount × (1 + rate)months ÷ 12
ICICI Home Finance, LIC Housing Finance, PNB Housing Finance: amount × (1 + rate)years × (1 + rate × months ÷ 12)
Shriram Finance: amount × (1 + monthly rate ÷ 12)months

The two banks work out interest day by day, in calendar-month parts, and add it to the deposit every quarter; Utkarsh pays simple interest on deposits of 180 days or less. With a payout, each payment counts the actual days in its period, on the company's payment dates, and is rounded to the rupee:

Interest = amount × payout rate × days ÷ 365 (366 for days in a leap year)

A full year at a yearly payout pays the rate itself, and the maturity date is the deposit date plus the tenure.

Worked Example

₹1,00,000 in LIC Housing Finance's Sanchay Public Deposit for 3 years, cumulative, at 7.20% p.a. (rates w.e.f. 1 Sep 2026): interest compounds once a year for the whole years, so the deposit grows to ₹1,23,193 by 16 Oct 2029, ₹23,193 of interest before tax. The monthly payout needs at least ₹2,00,000 and pays 6.95% p.a.: on ₹2,00,000 that is about ₹1,158 a month, each payment counted by the days in its month, and ₹41,700 in all.

Why Real Results Differ

  • Payment dates that fall on holidays move to a working day, and each company's own rounding can move a payment by a rupee.
  • A deposit gets the card in force on the day the company receives the money, and companies revise their cards.
  • Tax is deducted at source above the yearly threshold, and interest is taxed at your slab rate; the figures here are before tax.
  • Early withdrawal pays a lower rate, and a company FD is locked in for the first 3 months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I enter my own interest rate?

No. This calculator is for the FDs MoneyHoney offers: the rate comes from each company's card for the plan and investor type you choose.

Why does the first payment differ from the rest?

It covers the days from your deposit date to the company's first payment date, so it is shorter or longer than a full period.

Does it deduct TDS?

No. The figures are before tax. Tax is deducted at 10% once interest from a company passes ₹10,000 in a financial year, or ₹50,000 at a bank (₹1,00,000 for senior citizens), unless you submit Form 121.

Why is the maturity a rupee different from the company's card?

The card rounds its own way, and a bank's figure depends on the calendar days in each quarter, so the deposit date can move it by a rupee or two.
