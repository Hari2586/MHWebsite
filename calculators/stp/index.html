STP Calculator

A Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) places a lumpsum in one scheme, often a liquid or debt fund, and moves a fixed amount each month into another scheme of the same fund house. This calculator illustrates the balance in both schemes month by month, each at its own steady rate.

Source Fund Asset Class

Assumed 7% a year. For reference, the mean 10-year rolling return of the 10-year G-Sec, 1 Jun 2013 to 30 May 2023, was 7.20% (AMFI).

Target Fund Asset Class

Assumed 12% a year. For reference, the mean 10-year rolling return of the Nifty 50, 1 Jun 2013 to 30 May 2023, was 12.93% (AMFI).

How the STP Calculator Works

Two balances run side by side. Each month both schemes grow on what they held during the month; at the month end the transfer moves across (or whatever is left, once the source is nearly empty). Each rupee earns one month's growth at a time, in the scheme it sits in:

Sourcem = Sourcem−1 × (1 + is) − T
Targetm = Targetm−1 × (1 + it) + T

where T is the monthly transfer and is, it are the monthly rates of the source and target funds.

Worked Example

₹5,00,000 placed in a debt fund illustrated at 7%, with ₹25,000 moved each month into an equity fund illustrated at 12%. Month 1: the source grows to ₹5,02,917 and ₹25,000 moves across. Month 2: the target's ₹25,000 grows to ₹25,250 and receives another ₹25,000. The source is empty by month 22; after 3 years the target holds ₹6,83,948.

Why Real Results Differ

  • Markets do not grow at a steady rate: returns change every year and can be negative, and the order of good and bad years changes the outcome.
  • Each transfer is a redemption from the source fund, so it can attract capital gains tax and an exit load.
  • Both schemes must be from the same fund house, and many set a minimum number of transfers.
  • A scheme's returns are after its expense ratio, and a Regular plan costs more than the Direct plan of the same scheme.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why use an STP instead of investing everything at once?

An STP spreads the entry into the target scheme over several months. Whether that helps depends on how markets move in that time, which no calculator can know.

Is each transfer taxed?

Yes. A transfer redeems units of the source fund, so any gain on those units is taxed under that fund's tax class.

Can I transfer between fund houses?

No. STPs work only between schemes of the same fund house; moving money across fund houses needs a redemption and a fresh purchase.
