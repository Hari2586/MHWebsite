Tax

Residents cannot invest: only non-residents may hold this fund.

NRIs: only non-residents may hold this fund, so your gains on its units are exempt from Indian income tax (Income-tax Act, 2025, Schedule VI). Your country of residence may tax them.

HDFC says: “The Funds launched under Category III AIF at IFSC-GIFT City are subject to “Fund Level taxation in India”. Accordingly, the proceeds from Category III AIF should not be subject to tax in the hands of non-resident investors.” (presentation, Jul 2026, slide 7)

Important Information

GIFT City funds are run from GIFT IFSC, Gandhinagar, by fund managers registered with the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA). They are not mutual fund schemes regulated by SEBI. SEBI's mutual fund rules, the riskometer and SEBI SCORES do not apply to them.

Money Honey Financial Services Pvt Ltd is an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN-60930). We are not registered with IFSCA. We introduce GIFT City funds of the fund managers listed on this page, who pay us a commission when you invest through us. Ask us and we will tell you how much.

This page is general information about a GIFT City fund, not a recommendation or investment advice. It does not consider your goals, risk profile or tax position. Read the fund's placement memorandum before you invest. Past performance may or may not be sustained in future and is not a guarantee of any future returns.

This fund is in US dollars. Its value in rupees rises and falls with the exchange rate, as well as with the market.

This is a restricted scheme, offered by private placement only to accredited investors or to investors who invest at least USD 150,000. It is not available to everyone.

This fund is open only to non-residents. If you become resident in India, the fund's rules and the Income-tax Act require you to redeem: under the tax rule, within three months after the end of the tax year in which you become resident.

For a complaint about a fund, write first to its fund manager's complaints officer. If you are not satisfied, appeal within the fund house, then email IFSCA at grievance-redressal@ifsca.gov.in. For a complaint about our service, contact us.

Tax figures follow the Income-tax Act, 2025 as on 29 Sep 2026. Your tax depends on your residential status for the year and on any tax treaty. This is general information, not tax advice.
