Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a fund have no page?

We give a fund its own page only when its fund house publishes what it invests in, its minimum, who may invest, and a fund page or document. 40 of the 129 funds meet that. The rest link to their fund house's site; ask us about any of them.

What does “Status not published” mean?

The fund house does not say whether the fund is taking new money. Many private funds publish nothing about it; ask us and we will find out from the fund house.

Why are private funds hidden at first?

They are offered by private placement only, to accredited investors or to investors with at least USD 150,000. Choose “USD 150,000 or more” to see them.

Why are there no NAVs or returns?

We show what each fund is and who may invest, from its fund house's documents. Each fund links to its fund house's NAV and document pages, where they are published.

How up to date is this list?

We check it every month against IFSCA's directory and the fund houses' sites. This list is as on 10 Oct 2026.

Important Information

GIFT City funds are run from GIFT IFSC, Gandhinagar, by fund managers registered with the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA). They are not mutual fund schemes regulated by SEBI. SEBI's mutual fund rules, the riskometer and SEBI SCORES do not apply to them.

Money Honey Financial Services Pvt Ltd is an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN-60930). We are not registered with IFSCA. We introduce GIFT City funds of the fund managers listed on this page, who pay us a commission when you invest through us. Ask us and we will tell you how much.

This page is general information about GIFT City funds, not a recommendation or investment advice. It does not consider your goals, risk profile or tax position. Read a fund's offer document or placement memorandum before you invest.

Most GIFT City funds are in US dollars. Their value in rupees rises and falls with the exchange rate, as well as with the market.

Private funds are restricted schemes, offered by private placement only to accredited investors or to investors who invest at least USD 150,000. They are not available to everyone.

A GIFT City fund that takes residents pays tax on its own gains, and its NAV is shown after that tax. The offer documents warn that your redemption may also be taxed in your hands.

For a complaint about a fund, write first to its fund manager's complaints officer. If you are not satisfied, appeal within the fund house, then email IFSCA at grievance-redressal@ifsca.gov.in. For a complaint about our service, contact us.
