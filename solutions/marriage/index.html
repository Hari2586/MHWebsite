Why Wedding Budgets Keep Rising

  1. Gold Has Risen 4.5 Times in Ten Years

    The average price of gold in Mumbai rose from ₹26,534 per 10 grams in 2015-16 to ₹1,18,421 in 2025-26, and from ₹60,624 to ₹1,18,421 in the last two years alone.[1]

  2. Wedding Prices Rise Faster Than Most

    From 2015 to 2025, prices in general rose 4.71% a year and clothing 4.57%. Personal care and effects, which include gold and silver ornaments, rose 7.59% a year, and gold itself 14.08%. In the year to August 2026 alone, gold, diamond and platinum jewellery rose 35.5%.[2][3]

  3. Some Families Borrow for Ceremonies

    In 2019, loans taken for marriages and other ceremonies were 6.4% of what farm households owed, and 12.8% for households with the least land.[4]

  4. Marriage Comes Later, Leaving More Years to Save

    Women married at 23.1 on average in 2024, against 19.3 in 1990; in urban areas, at 24.4. More years between a child's birth and the wedding give a monthly SIP longer to grow.[5]

For some households, a child's wedding is the goal they invest for first. 8% of households name their child's marriage as their main goal for investing.[6]

How to Save Money for Marriage

  1. Price the wedding for the year it happens. At the calculator's numbers, a wedding costing ₹15,00,000 today costs ₹45,38,399 in 19 years, with prices rising 6% a year.
  2. Start the SIP that fills the gap. After what your savings could grow to, the gap is ₹45,38,399: a SIP of ₹5,185 a month at the rate you chose. It is an illustration, not a promise of any return.
  3. Start early: waiting costs. Starting 5 years later, with 14 years to go, needs ₹7,771 a month for the same wedding, about 1.5 times as much.
  4. Count the jewellery on its own. Bridal jewellery is an estimated 50% to 55% of India's gold jewellery demand by weight. This is an estimate by the World Gold Council, a private industry body, not official data.[7] Jewellery prices follow gold, which can rise or fall sharply in a year, so price it separately and allow for that.
  5. Lower the risk as the date nears. In the last few years, move the money from equity towards debt funds, so a market fall just before the wedding does less harm. The table below shows the categories by time left.
  6. Save rather than borrow at the end. RBI's average rate on new "other personal loans" was 10.41% in August 2026.[10] Every rupee saved ahead is a rupee not borrowed at that rate.
  7. Take money received at the wedding through the bank. No one may accept ₹2,00,000 or more in cash from one person for one event or occasion, and the penalty equals the amount. Take larger sums by bank transfer or cheque.[11]
  8. Raise the SIP as your income rises. The Step-Up SIP Calculator shows what raising it every year does.

Gold for Jewellery

Gold ETFs and gold funds of funds hold gold, or units that track its price, so their value moves with gold and can fall as well as rise. The rule-based Gold and Silver Funds screen lists them. Gold ETFs and gold funds of funds are taxed as "other" funds. Gains on units held up to 24 months (12 months for a listed ETF) are taxed at your slab rate; after that, at 12.5%, with no ₹1,25,000 exemption.[11]

The categories are described in general. This does not consider your goals, risk profile or tax position. Please speak to us for an assessment of suitability before investing, and read the scheme documents.

Wedding Money Questions People Ask

How much does a wedding cost in India?

No official body publishes it, and the two private sources differ widely. Couples who used a wedding website reported an average wedding budget of ₹39.5 Lakhs for 2025-26. This is a private survey of the site's users, mostly in cities, not official data.[8] A traders' body estimated ₹6.50 lakh crore of spending across 46 lakh weddings in the 2025 season, about ₹14.1 Lakhs a wedding. This is a private estimate, not official data.[9] The example uses ₹15,00,000 as an assumption: enter your own figure above.

Is ₹10 Lakhs enough for a wedding?

It depends on the wedding you plan and when. With prices rising 6% a year, a wedding that costs ₹10,00,000 today costs ₹30,25,600 in 19 years. Enter your own figures above.

How much should I save each month for my wedding?

In the Your Own Wedding example, a wedding costing ₹15,00,000 today and 3 years away needs ₹44,482 a month at an assumed 7% a year. The same wedding 19 years away needs ₹5,185 a month at 12%, because the money has much longer to grow.

Are wedding gifts taxed?

Gifts of money and property you receive on the occasion of your own marriage are not taxed, whoever gives them, and there is no limit. Gifts from relatives are not taxed at any time.[11] Based on income-tax rules in force on 29 Sep 2026. This is general information, not tax advice. Please consult a tax professional.

How much cash can I accept at a wedding?

No one may accept ₹2,00,000 or more in cash from one person for one event or occasion, and the penalty equals the amount. Take larger sums by bank transfer or cheque.[11]

What does a marriage loan cost?

A loan for a wedding is usually a personal loan. RBI's average rate on new "other personal loans" was 10.41% in August 2026.[10] At that rate, ₹5,00,000 repaid over 5 years costs about ₹1,43,480 in interest. The EMI Calculator works out any loan.

Can I save for wedding jewellery through gold funds?

Gold ETFs and gold funds of funds track the price of gold, so their value rises and falls with it; they do not fix today's price. Gold ETFs and gold funds of funds are taxed as "other" funds. Gains on units held up to 24 months (12 months for a listed ETF) are taxed at your slab rate; after that, at 12.5%, with no ₹1,25,000 exemption.[11]
