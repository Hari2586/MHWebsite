What a Trip Costs Today, and by the Time You Go

  1. More Indians Travel Abroad

    Indian nationals made 3.28 crore trips abroad in 2025, against 2.04 crore in 2015, and 43.41% of the trips were for a holiday.[1]

  2. Spending Abroad Has Nearly Doubled Since 2019

    Indians' spending on travel abroad fell to ₹92,925 Crores in 2020 and has risen every year since, to ₹3,09,715 Crores in 2025.[1]

  3. 2% Collected on Overseas Tour Packages

    From 1 Apr 2026, a tour operator in India collects 2% of the price of an overseas tour package as tax at source, from the first rupee. On a ₹2,00,000 package that is ₹4,000; until 31 Mar 2026 it was 5%, or ₹10,000.[4][5]

  4. What a Holiday Trip Costs at Home

    In 2025-26, an overnight holiday trip within India cost ₹11,723 on average for about 2 travellers, and ₹13,190 for an urban household.[2]

Most people pay for holidays from savings, but not all. In a 2024 survey of urban Indians planning a summer holiday, 86% meant to use their savings, 23% a credit card and 13% a loan. This is a private survey by Ipsos, not official data.[7]

How to Save for a Trip Before You Book

  1. Price the trip for the year you go. At the calculator's numbers, a trip costing ₹2,00,000 today costs ₹2,20,500 in 2 years, with prices rising 5% a year.
  2. Save the gap month by month. That is ₹8,536 a month at the rate you chose: an illustration, not a promise of any return.
  3. Keep short-term money out of equity. Equity can fall sharply within a year or two, so for a trip a few years away the table below lists debt and hybrid categories.
  4. Allow for the tax collected at source. From 1 Apr 2026, a tour operator in India collects 2% of the price of an overseas tour package as tax at source, from the first rupee.[4][5] Tax collected at source is a payment of your income tax, not an extra cost. It counts towards the tax you owe for the year, and you can claim back any excess in your return.[4]
  5. Pay with savings, not credit. RBI's average rate on new "other personal loans" was 10.41% in August 2026.[8] A trip repaid after it is over costs its price plus interest.
  6. Keep the emergency fund separate. Money for a trip is not money for emergencies: the Emergency Fund page works out how much to keep aside.
  7. Start the next trip's savings when you are back. The same monthly amount, kept going, pays for the next one.

Trip Savings Questions People Ask

How much should I save each month for a vacation?

At the calculator's numbers, a trip costing ₹2,00,000 today and 2 years away needs ₹8,536 a month. In the worked example, a ₹2,00,000 holiday abroad 2 years away needs ₹8,536 a month at an assumed 7% a year.

Does a SIP work for a short-term goal like a trip?

A SIP is a monthly investment, so it works if the money goes into a category that suits the time. For a trip 1 to 3 years away, the table above lists short duration and arbitrage funds; equity, whose value can fall sharply within a year or two, suits goals 5 years or more away.

What is TCS on an overseas tour package?

From 1 Apr 2026, a tour operator in India collects 2% of the price of an overseas tour package as tax at source, from the first rupee.[4][5] On the worked example's trip, that is about ₹4,410 on the price when you go.

Can I get back the TCS I paid on a foreign trip?

Tax collected at source is a payment of your income tax, not an extra cost. It counts towards the tax you owe for the year, and you can claim back any excess in your return.[4] Based on income-tax rules in force on 29 Sep 2026. This is general information, not tax advice. Please consult a tax professional.

What does an average holiday trip cost in India?

In 2025-26, an overnight holiday trip within India cost ₹11,723 on average for about 2 travellers, and ₹13,190 for an urban household.[2] Spending abroad came to about ₹94,000 per trip in 2025, counting trips for every purpose.[1]

Is travel now, pay later a loan?

Yes. Paying for a trip in instalments after you travel is credit, from a bank, a finance company or a card issuer, and it usually carries interest or fees. RBI's average rate on new "other personal loans" was 10.41% in August 2026.[8] Read the Key Facts Statement for the full cost before you sign.

How much can I send abroad for travel in a year?

A resident individual may send up to USD 2,50,000 abroad in a financial year under RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme.[6] Money sent abroad under the scheme other than for a tour package attracts tax at source of 20% once the year's total passes ₹10,00,000, or 2% for education or medical treatment.[4][5]
