Who can invest

RBI Bonds is the common name for the Government of India's Floating Rate Savings Bonds, 2020 (Taxable). The Government of India issues them, and the Reserve Bank of India manages the scheme through agency banks that act as Receiving Offices. They carry a sovereign guarantee on interest and repayment. The interest rate itself is not fixed and can go down.

  • Resident individuals, alone, jointly, or on an either-or-survivor basis.
  • A parent or legal guardian on behalf of a minor.
  • Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs).
  • Non-resident Indians cannot invest.
  • You need a PAN and a bank account. A demat account is not needed: the bonds are held in a Bond Ledger Account at the Receiving Office.

How the rate is set

The rate is the National Savings Certificate (NSC) rate plus 0.35%. It is reset on 1 Jan and 1 Jul, using the NSC rate the Government sets for the quarter that starts on that day, and applies to all holders, old and new, for the next six months. If the NSC rate falls, the bond rate falls with it.

Premature encashment

Before 7 years, you can encash the bonds only if you are 60 or over, and only after a lock-in that depends on your age on the day you encash.

  • Penalty: half of the last six months' interest.
  • Joint holding: at least one holder must meet the age rule.
  • Death of the holder: the lock-in follows the legal heir's age, not the nominee's.
  • All or nothing: an investment made in one application cannot be partly encashed.
  • You need proof of your date of birth. Check with your Receiving Office when the money is paid.

How to apply

  1. Enter the amount. From ₹1,000, in multiples of ₹1,000.
  2. Select or create your RBI Bond profile. Sign in to MoneyHoney, then fill in your investment and bank details, and your nominee.
  3. Approve the transaction. Review the prefilled application form and confirm.
  4. Pay by cheque. Account payee cheque in favour of HDFC Bank Floating Rate Savings Bonds 2020 (Taxable), submitted with the signed form. Submit it yourself at a Receiving Office branch, or MoneyHoney can collect it.
  5. Bonds are issued on the day the cheque is realised, the date of issue. The Receiving Office opens a Bond Ledger Account and sends a Certificate of Holding within 3 working days.

You will need your PAN, address proof and bank account proof, and proof of date of birth when investing for a minor.

Official pages. Read RBI's guidelines and the application form before applying.

Interest payment1 Jan and 1 Jul

Paid every six months on 1 Jan and 1 Jul, straight to your bank account, from the date of issue. The first payment covers the part-period from the date of issue to 30 Jun or 31 Dec, whichever comes first. Interest is counted on a 30/360 basis. There is no cumulative option: interest is always paid out. A payment due on a holiday is made on the next working day.

Maturity7 years

Repaid at par 7 years from the date of issue, with the interest for the last part-period. No interest accrues after maturity. The Receiving Office sends a reminder about a month before.

HoldingBond Ledger Account

Held only in electronic form, in a Bond Ledger Account at the Receiving Office, with one account per investor per office. A Certificate of Holding is issued within 3 working days of the funds being realised, and updated after each further investment.

Trading and loansNot tradable

The bonds cannot be traded in the secondary market and cannot be used as security for a loan from a bank, financial institution or NBFC.

Transfer and nominationNominee or heir only

The bonds cannot be transferred, except to a nominee or legal heir on the holder's death. A sole holder, or all joint holders, can nominate one or more nominees, resident or NRI, and split the amount between them in multiples of ₹1,000. Nomination is not available for a minor's holding.

Tax and TDSTaxable

Interest is taxable at your slab rate under the Income-tax Act, 2025. Tax is deducted at source at 10% when your interest from these bonds exceeds ₹10,000 in a financial year. If you are eligible, a declaration in Form 121 lets you receive interest without TDS. Tax and TDS

PaymentCheque, draft or online

By cheque, draft or an electronic transfer the Receiving Office accepts. Cash is accepted up to ₹20,000.

Where to applyReceiving Offices

Agency banks acting as Receiving Offices, such as SBI, the nationalised banks, IDBI Bank, Axis Bank, HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank, and Stock Holding Corporation of India. You can also invest online through RBI Retail Direct.

About the issuer

IssuerGovernment of India

Issued by the Government of India under notification F.No.4(10)-B(W&M)/2020 of 26 Jun 2020, and open on tap from 1 Jul 2020. The Reserve Bank of India manages the scheme under its operational guidelines of 2 Apr 2026 (RBI/2026-27/06), through agency banks acting as Receiving Offices.

ComplaintsReceiving Office

Take a complaint first to the Receiving Office that holds your Bond Ledger Account. If it does not reply within a month, or you are not satisfied with its reply, you can approach the Reserve Bank of India. MoneyHoney can help you raise it.

The interest rate is not fixed: it is reset every six months to the NSC rate plus 0.35% and can go down. The money is locked in for 7 years. Early encashment is allowed only for holders aged 60 or over, after a lock-in of 4 to 6 years depending on age, and costs half of the last six months' interest. The bonds cannot be sold, transferred or used as security for a loan. Interest is taxable at your slab rate, and tax is deducted at source above ₹10,000 a year. Tax laws can change.
