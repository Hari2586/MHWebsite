Who these bonds are for

These are tax-specific bonds, meant for investors claiming the Section 85 exemption on long-term capital gains from land or buildings, subject to its conditions.

  • You sold land, a building or both, held for more than 24 months.
  • You invest within 6 months of the sale, up to the amount of the gain.
  • Up to ₹50,00,000 in total across the tax year of the sale and the next tax year.
  • You can leave the money for 5 years: no sale, transfer or loan against the bonds.

Interest paymentYearly on 15 Oct

Paid once a year on 15 Oct by direct credit, from the day the issuer receives the money. Money received by 31 Aug earns its first interest on 15 Oct that year; money received later earns it on 15 Oct the next year, for the longer period. The last payment, at maturity, covers the part-year to the redemption date. A payment due on a holiday may be made on the adjacent working day, as the Information Memorandum sets out.

Coupon5.25%

5.25% a year, fixed for the life of the bonds, on money received during the series. The issuer may revise the rate for new applications by notice on its website.

Mode of issuePrivate placement

Private placement basis, on tap for the whole financial year. Base issue ₹1,000 Crores, with a green shoe option of up to ₹4,000 Crores.

Type of instrumentSecured, taxable

Secured, redeemable, non-convertible, non-cumulative, taxable bonds in the nature of debentures, with benefits under Section 85, Income-tax Act, 2025.

Issuance modePhysical or Demat

Physical (bond certificate) or Demat (NSDL or CDSL).

TransferabilityNot transferable

Non-transferable, non-marketable and non-negotiable, and cannot be offered as security for any loan or advance. A loan against the bonds within 5 years makes the exempted gain taxable. On the holder's death the bonds pass to the nominee or legal heir.

Redemption5 years, at par

At par, at the end of 5 years from the deemed date of allotment. There is no early redemption.

ListingNot listed

The bonds are not listed on any stock exchange, as they are not transferable. They are exempt from listing under SEBI's LODR Regulations.

TDS on interestNil for residents

None for resident investors (Notification 28/2018); deducted for non-resident investors. The interest is taxable either way.

Who can applyIndividuals, HUFs, firms and more

Individuals, HUFs, partnership firms, LLPs, companies and body corporates, banks, financial institutions, insurers, mutual funds, provident, superannuation and gratuity funds, FPIs, and NRIs from an NRO account on a non-repatriable basis. Minimum 2 bonds (₹20,000); maximum 500 bonds (₹50,00,000) in a financial year.

About company

Company profileIndian Railway Finance Corporation Limited

Indian Railway Finance Corporation Limited, incorporated on 12 December 1986 by the Ministry of Railways, is the market borrowing arm of Indian Railways. It raises money to acquire rolling stock and other assets, which it leases to the Ministry of Railways. It is a Public Financial Institution registered with the RBI as a non-banking financial company, and has been listed on NSE and BSE since 29 January 2021.

All past Capital Gain Bond series

These are tax-specific bonds, meant for investors claiming the Section 85 exemption on long-term capital gains from land or buildings, subject to its conditions.

Capital Gain Bonds are not bank deposits, are not insured by DICGC and are not guaranteed by the Government of India. They carry the issuer's credit risk, and ratings can change. They are locked in for 5 years, cannot be transferred, pledged or sold, and are not listed. The interest is taxable. The exemption depends on meeting the conditions of Section 85, and tax laws can change. The issuer may revise the coupon or close the issue early. Consult a tax adviser.

MoneyHoney is an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor. Capital Gain Bonds are not mutual fund products, and AMFI and SEBI grievance mechanisms do not apply to them; grievances lie with the issuer.
