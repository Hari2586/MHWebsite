How to Read This

Rolling returns remove start date luck: every possible entry date is one observation, and every selected strategy is measured over the same set of dates so the observation counts match. Longer holding windows squeeze the range of outcomes; watch the minimum column and the loss probability fall as the window grows.
Drawdown is the fall from the running all time high (ATH) within the chosen window; 0% means the scheme is at a new high that day. It answers the question investors actually feel: how deep was the worst dip, and how long did it take to climb out?

SIP Versus Traditional Options

Monthly SIP Ledger
Post Office and SBI recurring deposits compound at their published rates. Gold uses the domestic price series. Every option receives the same instalments on the same dates, so the finishing values are directly comparable.

Top-up SIP (also called step-up or incremental SIP) raises the monthly amount by a fixed percentage once a year, the same way a salary rises. The plan keeps pace with income instead of staying frozen at the amount chosen on day one.

The XIRR of the top-up plan is usually a touch lower because more of its money arrives in later years. What matters to the household is the wealth row: the bigger cheques land exactly when the family can afford them.

Monthly SIP Amount, Year by Year

Bull market: when the broad market like SENSEX goes up or rallies higher. Bear market: when the broad market like SENSEX goes down.

There are broadly 3 possibilities of returns when you invest through SIP:

  1. Negative returns when you invest only in the bear phase and withdraw money immediately after a run of negative returns.
  2. Positive returns when you invest throughout the bull and bear phase.
  3. Positive returns when you invest only in the bear phase but do not withdraw money.
